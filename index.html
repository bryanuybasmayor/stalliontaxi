How it works

01

Three steps,
no app.

Book by phone in under a minute. If you send a request from this page, we still call or text to lock in the time before the car leaves.

  1. STEP 01

    Call or send a request

    Ring (306) 920-9896, or fill in the booking form with your pickup, drop-off and time.

  2. STEP 02

    We confirm your pickup

    You get a call or text back to confirm the time, the price for out-of-town trips, and anything you need a hand with.

  3. STEP 03

    Your driver arrives

    Meet the car at the address you gave us. If plans change on the way, call and we will sort it out.
